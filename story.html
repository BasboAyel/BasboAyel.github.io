Curiosity
with consequences.

I’m a computer scientist, bioinformatician, and aspiring HCI researcher who believes the best tools are built with people—not merely for them.

A little context

My work moves between rooms that rarely overlap: a field site collecting environmental data, a computational biology lab, a makerspace with assistive prototypes, and the software systems that make research useful day-to-day.

That movement is intentional. The problems I care about—health, access, and human-centered science—need people who can investigate the data and listen deeply to the people it represents.

“The most important expertise in a design room belongs to the people who will use what is being made.”— a principle I try to carry into every project
